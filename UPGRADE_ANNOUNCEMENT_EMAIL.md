## 🎉 What's New

### 1. **XDMails - Bulk Email Campaign Manager** 📧

Send professional marketing and communication emails to multiple recipients with ease!

**Key Features:**

- ✅ **Rich Text Editor** - Full formatting with bold, italic, headings, lists, and more
- ✅ **Recipient Management** - Upload CSV/TXT/XLS/XLSX files or add emails manually
- ✅ **Media Library** - Upload and insert images and videos directly into emails
- ✅ **Campaign Tracking** - Monitor sent, draft, and failed campaigns with detailed statistics
- ✅ **Professional Templates** - Branded AHS email templates with responsive design
- ✅ **Gmail Optimized** - Emojis and formatting render perfectly across all email clients

**Who Can Access:** Superadmin, Admin, Operations

**How to Use:**

1. Navigate to **XDMails** in the sidebar
2. Click **"New Campaign"** in the XDComposer tab
3. Add subject, compose your message, and upload recipient list
4. Click **"Save & Queue"** to send

---

### 2. **XDDrive - Financial Document Manager** 📁

Organize all your receipts, invoices, and financial documents in one secure location!

**Key Features:**

- ✅ **Folder Organization** - Create folders with custom colors for easy categorization
- ✅ **Document Upload** - Support for PDF, images, and Office documents (up to 10MB)
- ✅ **Document Types** - Tag documents as Receipt, Invoice, Statement, Contract, Report, or Other
- ✅ **Preview & Download** - View documents directly in the browser or download them
- ✅ **Search Functionality** - Quickly find documents by name or description
- ✅ **Grid & List Views** - Choose your preferred viewing mode
- ✅ **Nested Folders** - Create subfolders for better organization

**Who Can Access:** Superadmin, Admin, Finance, CEO

**How to Use:**

1. Navigate to **XDDrive** in the sidebar
2. Click **"New Folder"** to create organized categories (e.g., "2026 Receipts", "Invoices")
3. Click **"Upload"** to add documents with name, type, and description
4. Browse, preview, and manage your documents with ease
