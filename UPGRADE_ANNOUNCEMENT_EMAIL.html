We're excited to announce a major upgrade to the AHS-XD Executive Dashboard! Version 2.7 is now live with powerful new features designed to streamline your workflow and boost productivity.

📧 XDMails - Bulk Email Campaign Manager

Send professional marketing and communication emails to multiple recipients with ease!

Key Features:

  • ✅ Rich Text Editor - Full formatting with bold, italic, headings, lists
  • ✅ Recipient Management - Upload CSV/TXT/XLS/XLSX or add manually
  • ✅ Media Library - Insert images and videos into emails
  • ✅ Campaign Tracking - Monitor sent, draft, and failed campaigns
  • ✅ Gmail Optimized - Perfect rendering across all email clients

Access: Superadmin, Admin, Operations

📁 XDDrive - Financial Document Manager

Organize all your receipts, invoices, and financial documents in one secure location!

Key Features:

  • ✅ Folder Organization - Create folders with custom colors
  • ✅ Document Upload - PDF, images, Office docs (up to 10MB)
  • ✅ Document Types - Tag as Receipt, Invoice, Statement, etc.
  • ✅ Preview & Download - View documents in browser
  • ✅ Search Functionality - Find documents quickly
  • ✅ Grid & List Views - Choose your preferred view

Access: Superadmin, Admin, Finance, CEO
